## Optional supplementary narration

“When a temperature plot slopes upward, the next question is whether that estimate depends on the period or the assumptions. Verdict Earth uses NASA POWER’s MERRA-2-derived 2-meter air temperature at eight distinct Sindh grid cells. It weights every valid monthly mean by its calendar days and uses complete years to estimate trends.

“Select Karachi. From 1981 to 2025, the fit is about a tenth of a degree Celsius per decade. Switch to 2001–2025 and the fitted slope is +0.116. Conventional OLS gives an interval that includes zero. Newey–West accounts for short-range dependence, and here its interval is narrower and excludes zero. Switching the method keeps the observations and the fitted line the same.

“Use a second location to compare the curves on shared years. The point samples represent reanalysis cells, and the comparison is descriptive. In How We Know, inspect the original NASA response, spatial resolution, assumptions, and limitations. Download the selected results with the underlying provenance. Don’t just detect a trend. Test whether it survives scrutiny.”
