# 29-second demo and storyboard

Record at **29 seconds** with English captions. Rehearse opening **Case Studies → Karachi → Checks**. The two preset links open the matching Evidence Lab verdict.

| Time | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Show World Scan and the **Global Evidence Stress Test**. | “Verdict Earth lets you test NASA temperature trends against your analysis choices.” |
| 5–12 s | In the Karachi case’s **Checks** step, select **Inspect 1981–2025 verdict**. | “Karachi’s NASA POWER record trends up about 0.11 degrees per decade. Both nominal intervals exclude zero.” |
| 12–20 s | Use Back to return to the case, then choose **Inspect 2001–2025 verdict**. Show the verdict and the interval table. | “The shorter window shows almost the same slope. Now the conventional and Newey–West intervals disagree.” |
| 20–29 s | Show the coverage audit, the report download, and the Giovanni comparison under **Methods & Sources → Giovanni**. | “Inspect the assumptions, download the evidence, and trace the record. Don’t just detect a trend. Test whether it survives scrutiny.” |
