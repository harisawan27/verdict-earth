# 29-second demo and storyboard

Record at **29 seconds** with English captions. Use a wide view so the selected grid coordinates, trend, and chart stay legible.

| Time | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0–7 s | Open the homepage on **World Scan**. Search for **Karachi** and select it to reveal the geographic context and grid-cell center. | “Explore 45 years of NASA GISTEMP across 15,875 grid cells. Search Karachi to find its grid cell.” |
| 7–13 s | Show the annual anomaly chart and 1951–1980 reference. | “These are smoothed surface temperature anomalies. Each cell has its own fitted trend.” |
| 13–22 s | Switch to **Local Investigations · POWER**, then open Karachi’s **Evidence Lab** and select **2001–2025**. | “Local Investigations uses a separate NASA POWER record. For Karachi, two uncertainty methods read the same fitted slope differently.” |
| 22–29 s | Show the two intervals and the Giovanni comparison under **How We Know**. | “Check the intervals, then trace the Karachi comparison to NASA Giovanni. This is Verdict Earth.” |
