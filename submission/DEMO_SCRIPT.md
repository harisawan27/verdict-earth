# 29-second demo and storyboard

Record at **29 seconds** with English captions. Open the page on the guided Karachi case and rehearse the two preset links.

| Time | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Show World Scan and the **Global Evidence Stress Test**. | “Verdict Earth lets you test NASA temperature trends against your analysis choices.” |
| 5–12 s | Open **Guided case** and select **1. Inspect 1981–2025**. | “Karachi’s NASA POWER record trends up about 0.11 degrees per decade. Both nominal intervals exclude zero.” |
| 12–20 s | Return to **Guided case** and select **2. Inspect 2001–2025**. Show the verdict and the interval table. | “The shorter window shows almost the same slope. Now the conventional and Newey–West intervals disagree.” |
| 20–29 s | Show the coverage audit, the report download, and the Giovanni source under **How We Know**. | “Inspect the assumptions, download the evidence, and trace the record. Don’t just detect a trend. Test whether it survives scrutiny.” |
